## Description

Friends, profiles, book sharing and buddy reading. Books may be shared only 1:1 with an accepted friend, by server-side copy, with notice-and-takedown in place before sharing ships. No public or link-based book sharing. See ADR-0004 (backlog/decisions/ADR-0004-friends-only-book-sharing.md).
