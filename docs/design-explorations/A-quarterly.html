Lesefluss takes an EPUB, PDF, HTML, plain text, or any URL, pulls a clean reading copy, and gives you back the words. You read them in pages, as a scroll, or one at a time.

The one-at-a-time mode is called RSVP. Each word lands on the same spot — readfasterno eyejumps — so your eyes don't have to jump back. Many people read 2 to 4× faster like that. It feels weird for a minute, then it doesn't.

Browse 80,000+ free classics from Standard Ebooks, Project Gutenberg, or follow web novels straight from Royal Road, AO3, ScribbleHub, Wuxiaworld. Or paste any link and read it.

§ 3 · reader

Small dials, not big switches.

Three themes: light, dark, sepia. Pick a font, line spacing, margin, even the size of the app itself. Tune WPM, punctuation pauses, acceleration, focal-letter offset. Tap any word for a dictionary entry. Save passages with colored highlights. Keep a per-book glossary that lights up names and places as they appear. When a chapter ends, the next one rolls in.

All of it works offline. Sign in if you want your library on every device; don't if you don't.

§ 4 · everywhere

Web, Android, and a small device.

Same library, three surfaces. Read in a browser tab. Read on Android, offline. Or build the optional €25 ESP32 reader — AMOLED or TFT, one button, weeks of battery, BLE-synced to your phone.

There's also a Chrome extension that saves whatever article you're looking at straight to your library. One click. Firefox version coming.
